Wristful

A quiet meditation timer you feel rather than hear.

Taps you can count

Wristful times your meditation on Apple Watch and marks it with silent taps on your wrist: one for an interval, two for the next round, three and a soft bell at the end. There's nothing to look at, so it fits a quiet room and a group.

Plan on the iPhone, sit with the watch

Set up your meditations on the iPhone: the length, a cue every few minutes, rounds of sitting and walking. Start one from the iPhone or the watch, by asking Siri, from a complication, or with the Action button on Apple Watch Ultra.

Every sitting kept

Each meditation goes to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes and, if you like, to Apple Fitness as a Mind & Body workout. History shows each one with your heart rate, and your week in plain numbers: no streaks, no scores.

A gentle reminder

Reminders at the times you choose, which stay quiet on a day you've already meditated. One touch starts your usual meditation on the watch.

Calm and private

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your meditations stay on your iPhone and Apple Watch, and in Apple Health.
